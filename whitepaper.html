6. Transparency

SOFI aims to communicate project-controlled activity, important wallet information and major changes openly. No team member, admin or community representative should guarantee returns or private access to profits.

The project may maintain resources for legitimate activities such as marketing, listings, website development, creative work, competitions and community operations. Their use should be communicated responsibly whenever relevant.

Community members should independently review on-chain information and avoid relying solely on promotional messages.

7. Community participation

SOFI encourages memes, replies, reposts, comments, raids and original creative content. Engagement should be authentic: no bots, impersonation, harassment, misleading claims or spam.

Community campaigns and rewards may have separate eligibility rules, deadlines and verification requirements. Participation never guarantees a reward unless the published rules are met and verified.

8. Marketing & visibility

The project may pursue organic social growth, community campaigns, public token profiles, market-data integrations, listing applications and selective collaborations. Third-party platforms independently decide whether to display, verify or list a token.

SOFI does not endorse unsolicited marketers who privately request payment. Official marketing arrangements, when used, should be evaluated carefully and announced through verified channels where appropriate.

9. Risk disclosure

SOFI is a meme token created for entertainment and community purposes. Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile, liquidity can change rapidly and participants may lose all funds used to acquire tokens.

Nothing in this Litepaper, on the website or in community channels is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Information may change as the project develops. Always verify official links, protect your wallet, avoid sharing seed phrases and conduct your own research before interacting with any crypto asset.
